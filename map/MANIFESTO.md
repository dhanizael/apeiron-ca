# APEIRON
## A Digital Universe of 0 and 1, with a Law-Discovering Machine Inside It

**Manifesto v1 — 2026-09-24**

We started from one question: *can a universe made of nothing but bits
discover its own laws — and can the discovery be verified exactly, because
we hold the source code?*

This project is the answer under construction. It has two halves in one
closed loop:

1. **Semesta** — a digital universe: a family of mass-conserving cellular
   automata (the *flow family*), k-bit cells on a ring, conservation by
   construction, deterministic to the bit.
2. **Newton** — a machine that watches the universe's raw state stream and
   recovers its laws exactly (microscopic tables, macroscopic flux curves),
   then *intervenes* — and every intervention is verified against ground
   truth, because we hold the source code.

The loop is closed: nothing enters from outside. The loop is open-ended:
every discovery forces a new intervention, every intervention forces the
world to show a face it never had.

## What we have already done (all reproducible; see STATUS.md)

- Recovered laws **exactly** from raw states — 4096/4096 entries, macro
  flux curve with held-out MAE 0.0.
- Built an engine at **2.31×10⁹ cell-updates/second** (frozen benchmark
  protocol, bit-identical across thread counts and implementations).
- Found universes we never planted; recovered their complete laws.
- Proved a **conservation ceiling** (flux ≤ mass density, digit-precise)
  and rode it instead of breaking it.
- Verified a **reaction-front replicator** — one seed converts the entire
  universe into a crystal of itself — with controls (4/4 seeds, unseeded
  control yields zero).
- Found a **perpetuum ecologis** — a 10-species universe whose populations
  never settle, with an anti-phase (predation-signature) oscillation —
  the first of our worlds that *never finishes*.
- Built **logic gates** inside it — a regenerative OR and a reusable,
  self-restoring AND — and wired two gates into a 3-input circuit,
  verified on full truth tables.
- Ran an **open-endedness meter**: our best world's model had *not
  saturated* by 10⁶ steps — the first world whose model was still growing
  at the horizon.

## The question this manifesto keeps open

*Is true Open-Ended Evolution possible in a closed universe of fixed
dimension?* Our answer, after 24 research logs: **not impossible —
conditional.** Three walls, three different answers:

1. **The pigeonhole wall** — a finite state space guarantees eventual
   recurrence. For our universe: ~10¹⁹⁷⁰⁰ times the age of the universe.
   The wall exists and is irrelevant.
2. **The model wall (measured)** — a fixed law is a finite object; a
   model that merely covers it saturates. Our older worlds saturate;
   measured, in bits.
3. **The depth wall** — a fixed finite world can still hold inexhaustible
   *descriptions* (Gödel's move: the theory of a fixed structure is not
   exhausted by a fixed language). If the dynamics supports unbounded
   composition — if it is, in Turing's sense, a computer — the model grows
   for any horizon that matters. We have measured the three ingredients
   (memory, signal, interaction) inside one world; the gates are the first
   compositions. Whether the flow family is computationally universal at
   some fixed k is our sharpest open question — and it is testable.

And the loop itself: the composite of universe + discovery machine is
closed (no external input) and has already produced eighteen rounds of
genuine novelty in fact. The openness lives in the relation.
