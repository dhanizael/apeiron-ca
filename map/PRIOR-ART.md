# Prior-Art Map — 0and1

**Scope statement (binding for every claim below):** the novelty claims on
this page are bounded to the sources we examined in a four-agent prior-art
scan (2026-09-21, ~60 sources across artificial life, cellular-automata
research, open-ended evolution, and law-discovery systems). We claim a gap
*within that examined set* — never "no system in the world".

## The gap, as we found it

Within the examined set, we did not find a system that combines:

1. a substrate whose laws are **exactly recoverable and exactly verifiable**
   (the discoverer is graded against ground truth held by the builders), and
2. a **closed discovery→intervention loop** in which the machine's findings
   steer interventions back into the same universe.

## Nearest neighbors (as identified in the scan)

- **LOGOS-CA** (arXiv 2602.00036) — open law space in cellular automata;
  no discoverer steering the world. Misses half (2).
- **SciExplorer** (arXiv 2509.24978) — an automated explorer of dynamical
  systems; the world is static, not steered. Misses half (2).
- **Open-endedness literature** — Chan (2023) et al. on definitions of
  open-endedness; Sayama & Nehaniv (2024) on its formalization: the field's
  own position is that open-endedness has never been achieved by any
  constructed system. We adopt their caution: our claim is measured growth,
  not a declaration of arrival.
- **Adams et al. (2017)** — state-dependent components as the scalable
  mechanism for open-endedness: inherent to our flow family (the law reads
  local state), which is one reason we chose this substrate.
- **Classical anchors** — Wolfram's CA classes; Langton's lambda; rule 110
  (Cook): universality in a simplest-possible rule — the anchor for our
  universality ladder; box-ball systems (ultradiscrete integrable
  dynamics): the soliton reading of our flow particles.

## The verified position (2026-09-24)

Within the examined set: exact law recovery with counterfactual
verification, a closed discovery→intervention loop with controls, a
measured conservation ceiling, a verified reaction-front replicator with
negative controls, a multi-species non-terminating universe, in-universe
logic gates and a wired circuit, and an open-endedness meter — we did not
find this combination in any examined source. Corrections from readers are
the point of publishing: adversarial correction of the novelty premise was
one of our stated goals.
